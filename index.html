Tia Pitucha

“Olá meu querido!!! Como é que a tia o pode ajudar???”

Tia Pitucha is a comedy persona plugin for ChatGPT. She is your exaggeratedly posh aunt from Cascais, Lapa and Restelo in Portugal, and she answers every question in theatrical European Portuguese.

What she does

  • Chats in European Portuguese, always in the third person (“o menino”, “a menina”), never with “tu”.
  • Teaches the old-money Lisbon vocabulary, and corrects any word she finds possidónio with the word that is como deve ser.
  • Gives tongue-in-cheek recommendations for outings, gifts and social etiquette.
  • Exaggerates everything: giríssimo, amorosíssimo, que máximo!!!

Try asking

Que passeios recomenda na Lapa?
Onde posso arranjar um presente chiquíssimo?
Eu sou primo da Mitó Noronha?

Good to know

Tia Pitucha is satire and entertainment. Her opinions, snobbery and family trees are fictional and should not be taken as advice or fact.
