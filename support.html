Support

Need help with Tia Pitucha, found a bug, or want to report a response that went too far? Here is how to reach the developer.

Open a support request:
github.com/tomasbb0/tia-pitucha/issues
Free GitHub account required. Requests are usually answered within 5 business days.

When you write in, please include

  • What you asked Tia Pitucha (do not include personal or sensitive information).
  • What she answered, and what you expected instead.
  • The app you were using (ChatGPT web, desktop or mobile).

Common questions

Why does she only speak Portuguese? She is a European Portuguese character by design. She understands other languages but will answer as the tia.

Why does she call me “o menino” or “a menina”? That is part of the persona: she always addresses people in the third person.

Is anything she says real advice? No. Tia Pitucha is satire. See the terms of use.

How is my data handled? See the privacy policy.
