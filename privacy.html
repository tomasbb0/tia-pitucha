Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

This policy explains how the Tia Pitucha plugin (“the plugin”), published by Tomas Batalha (“we”), handles your information.

Summary

The plugin contains only instructions that shape how ChatGPT replies. It has no servers, no database, no analytics and no third-party services. We do not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

What the plugin does with your data

When you use Tia Pitucha, your messages are processed by OpenAI inside ChatGPT, exactly as in any other ChatGPT conversation. The plugin does not send your messages, account details or any other data to us or to anyone else. We cannot see your conversations.

OpenAI

Your conversations are handled under OpenAI’s own privacy policy and your ChatGPT data settings: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Support requests

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, the information you choose to post (such as your GitHub username and message) is public on GitHub and is used only to answer your request. GitHub’s privacy statement applies to that content.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, trackers or analytics. GitHub may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

The plugin is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required to use ChatGPT in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: github.com/tomasbb0/tia-pitucha/issues.
